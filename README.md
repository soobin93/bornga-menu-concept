# Bornga Table Order — concept

An unsolicited redesign concept for the QR table-ordering app at
**Bornga Korean BBQ, Brisbane** (82 Colmslie Rd is the other venue; Bornga is in the CBD).

Open it on a phone: **https://soobin93.github.io/bornga-menu-concept/**

## What this is

A working prototype of the ordering flow, not a finished product. Every feature of the
restaurant's current app is present: the full 126-item menu, categories, search, item detail,
upsells, cart, order history, and the two-meat house rule surfaced as live order state.

## Notes

- Menu data and dish photography belong to Bornga. They were taken from the restaurant's own
  live ordering page and are reproduced here only to show the redesign in context.
- Photos were cut from their white backgrounds programmatically. A handful carry promotional
  graphics burned into the original files and would need retouching from the source.
- Item descriptions were truncated at ~55 characters in the source data and have been completed
  for the prototype. Final copy should come from the restaurant.
